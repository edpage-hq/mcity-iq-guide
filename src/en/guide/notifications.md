# Notifications

M'City IQ tells you about what concerns you: in the app and, if you wish, by
e-mail. You are never notified of your own actions.

## The bell

The bell in the header shows the number of unread notifications and the ten
latest. It refreshes on its own every minute.

- Click a notification to open it: it is marked as read and takes you to the
  page concerned.
- **Mark all as read** clears the counter.
- **See all notifications** opens the Notifications page.

## The Notifications page

It lists all your notifications, with the **All** and **Unread** filters and a
link to the **Settings**. Notifications are shown in your working language.
Those read more than 90 days ago are deleted automatically.

## What you are notified of

| Event                   | Who is notified                                                             | E-mail by default |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Communications          | The recipients of a published communication                                 | No                |
| Shared resources        | The accounts whose scope covers the resource                                | No                |
| Contact messages        | Those who handle messages (CEO, Support head, Support L1)                   | Yes               |
| Desynchronized products | The CEO and the members of the product's teams                              | Yes               |
| Indicator collection    | After three failures in a row: the CEO and the product's teams              | Yes               |
| Teams                   | The account added to or removed from a team                                 | No                |
| Your access             | The account whose position, department, scope or back-office access changes | Yes               |

A desynchronized-product alert also shows as a banner on the desk.

## Choosing how you are notified

In **Settings → Notifications**, tick **In the app** and/or **By e-mail** for
each event, then save. The list shows only the events your position can
receive.
