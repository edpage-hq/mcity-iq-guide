# Frequently asked questions

## I didn't receive my invitation

Check your spam folder. Otherwise, ask the administrator to
[resend it](./administration/accounts): the new e-mail carries a new temporary
password, and the old one stops working.

## My temporary password no longer works

It has expired (7 days by default). Ask the administrator to resend the
invitation.

## I don't receive the sign-in code

Wait a minute, check your spam folder, then click **Send a new code**. The code
is valid for 15 minutes. If e-mails still don't arrive, contact the
administrator.

## I can't see a product

Your scope doesn't cover it. Ask your head of unit or the administrator to add
it to your account or your team.

## The intranet works, but not a product's back-office

Access to a product's back-office is granted separately. Ask the administrator
for it (see [Accounts](./administration/accounts)). For Auticway, see also the
[refusal messages](./auticway#refusal-messages).

## I can't attach a zone to a product

Only qualified zones can be attached. Qualify the zone in the
[registry](./administration/registry#zones), or ask Product Operations.

## I lost my authenticator phone

Use a recovery code to sign in, then disable the app in
[My account](./account#security). Without a recovery code, contact the
administrator.

## I get too many e-mails

For each event, choose between the notification and the e-mail in your
[preferences](./notifications#choosing-how-you-are-notified).

## Contacts

For a question this guide doesn't answer, contact your head of unit or the
platform's general administrator. Their details are in the intranet's shared
resources.
