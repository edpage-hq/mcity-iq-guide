# My account

Open the menu under your name, bottom left, then **Settings**. The settings
have five tabs: Profile, Security, Notifications, Appearance and Language.

## Profile

Change your name and e-mail address, and add a profile photo. Changing your
e-mail address requires confirming the new one through the link received.

Your position, scope and access are not changed here: they belong to the
general administrator.

## Security

### Password

Enter the current password, then the new one (at least 12 characters by
default). Changing your password forgets all your remembered devices.

### Authenticator app

1. **Enable 2FA**, then confirm your password.
2. Scan the QR code with your app (or enter the setup key).
3. Enter the 6-digit code shown by the app.
4. Keep the **recovery codes** in a password manager: each one allows one
   sign-in if you lose your phone.

Once the app is active, it gives you the sign-in code instead of the e-mail.
**Disable 2FA** goes back to the e-mail code if two-factor authentication is
required for you.

### Passkeys

**Add passkey** registers a passkey (fingerprint, face, phone PIN or security
key) under a name of your choice. You then sign in without a password or code.
Remove a passkey you no longer use.

### Remembered devices

The list shows the browsers on which you ticked "Remember this device" while
entering a code received by e-mail: they don't ask for a code for 30 days.
**Forget** removes a device; **Forget all** removes them all. Do it if you lose
a device or used a shared computer.

## Notifications

See [Notifications](./notifications#choosing-how-you-are-notified).

## Appearance

Choose the light theme, the dark theme or your system's.

## Language

Choose English or French. The language applies to the whole intranet, the
M'City back-office and your notifications.
