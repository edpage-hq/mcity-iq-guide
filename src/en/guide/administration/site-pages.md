# Site pages

**Administration → Site pages** edits the public site's content, without
development. Open to the CEO and to Partner & Customer Support.

## The pages

| Page                                       | Content                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| General                                    | Header and footer: name, tagline, legal identity, address, e-mail, phone, social networks      |
| Home                                       | Title, introduction, pillars, products and zones sections, calls to action, frequent questions |
| About us                                   | Vision, mission, values, regional roots, legal identity                                        |
| Our products                               | Introduction to the product list, "Next product" card                                          |
| Our zones                                  | Approach, method, note below the zones                                                         |
| Services, Partners                         | Items, steps, partnership types                                                                |
| Press & media kit                          | Presentation, figures, key messages, logo, media kit, press contact                            |
| Careers                                    | Culture, job openings, how to apply                                                            |
| Contact                                    | Text above the form, confirmation message                                                      |
| Legal notice, Privacy policy, Terms of use | Text sections, last-updated date                                                               |

Product landing pages are not here: their content is entered on the product
page, in the [registry](./registry#registering-a-product). The "Our zones" page
lists the registry's qualified zones.

## Editing a page

1. Open the page from the list. The list shows the last change and its author;
   **View on site** opens the public page.
2. Choose the **editing language**. English is needed for a text to show;
   French falls back to English when empty.
3. Edit the fields. In a list (pillars, openings, questions…), add, remove or
   reorder items.
4. Save.

The change is live immediately and recorded in the [audit log](./logs).
