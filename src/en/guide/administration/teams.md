# Teams

A team is a group of named accounts responsible for one or more products.
**Administration → Teams** is for the CEO only.

## What a team gives

Each member of a team responsible for a product, whatever their position:

- sees the product in their scope and in the intranet;
- opens its back-office through SSO, without access granted account by account;
- gets the alert when the product is desynchronized, or when its indicators can
  no longer be collected.

An account can belong to several teams.

## Creating or editing a team

1. **New team** (or open an existing team).
2. **Name** in English (required) and French, optional **department**.
3. **Members**: search for accounts and add them.
4. Save.

Members added or removed are notified. A team is not deleted.

## Making a team responsible for a product

Teams are chosen on the product page, in the [registry](./registry), under
**Responsible teams**.

## Removing a member or a team

Removing a member from a team, or a team from a product, revokes the account's
sessions and tokens for the back-offices it loses, unless it has access some
other way (granted access or another team).

All these changes are recorded in the [audit log](./logs).
