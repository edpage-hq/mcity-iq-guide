# Contact messages

Messages sent from the public site's contact form arrive in a shared queue, open
to the CEO, the Support head and every Support L1, with no filter by product or
zone.

## What a message holds

- The sender's name and e-mail. The form creates no account and is protected by
  a CAPTCHA.
- **Subject**: general question, partnership, press, careers or other.
- The **product** concerned, when the message comes from a product's landing
  page.
- Up to two **attachments** (PDF or Word, 2 MB each, e.g. a CV and a cover
  letter). They are never public: only those who handle messages download them
  from the queue.

## Handling the queue

1. **Administration → Messages**. Filter by status or subject.
2. Open a message, reply to the sender by e-mail or route it to the right
   person.
3. Change its **status**: **New**, **In progress**, then **Closed**. The message
   shows who handled it.

Each new message is notified, and e-mailed by default, to those who handle the
queue. The desk also shows the number of new messages.
