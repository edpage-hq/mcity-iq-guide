# Communications and resources

Open to the CEO and to Partner & Customer Support.

## Communications

A communication is an announcement published in the intranet, visible as soon
as it is saved.

1. **Administration → Communications → New communication**.
2. Enter the **title** and **message**, and choose the language it is **written
   in** (English or French).
3. Choose the **recipients**:

   | Recipients        | Who receives it                                                 |
   | ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
   | All of M'City     | Every account                                                   |
   | Product           | Accounts whose scope covers the product                         |
   | Zone              | Accounts assigned the zone, or with an office in the zone       |
   | Office            | Accounts assigned the office (HR and Finance cover all offices) |
   | Department        | Accounts attached to the department                             |
   | Position          | Accounts with that position                                     |
   | Chosen recipients | The selected accounts                                           |

4. Choose one or more **targets**: an account receives the communication if its
   assignment matches at least one of them. Management (CEO and heads) covers
   all products, zones and offices.
5. **Publish**.

Recipients are told through a [notification](../notifications). Those who
publish can edit or delete a communication; deleting it removes it from every
recipient's intranet.

## Shared resources

**Administration → Resources** manages the intranet's documents, procedures,
notes, contacts and links.

1. **New resource**.
2. Choose the **type** and enter the **title** and **text**, in English and
   French.
3. Depending on the type:
   - document: attach the **file** (PDF, office document, text or image, 2 MB at
     most);
   - link: enter the **link address**.
4. **Product**: leave **General (all of M'City)** for a resource everyone sees,
   or choose an active product to keep it for the accounts whose scope covers
   it. It then also appears in the product's space.
5. **Create the resource**.

The accounts concerned are notified. Deleting a resource also deletes its file,
for good. A file can be downloaded only after the reader's scope is checked.
