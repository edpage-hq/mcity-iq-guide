# Offices and departments

The organization's referentials are for the CEO only. They are not deleted.
Zones belong to the [registry](./registry#zones) and teams have
[their own page](./teams).

## Offices

**Administration → Offices** lists M'City's physical locations.

1. **New office**.
2. Enter the name and choose the **zone** it is in.
3. **Create office**.

Offices are used in the scope of Support L1 and L2 accounts, and as a target for
communications. Targeting a zone also reaches the accounts with an office in
that zone.

## Departments

**Administration → Departments** lists M'City's units, with the number of
accounts attached.

1. **New department**.
2. Enter the name.
3. **Create department**.

An account is attached to a department from its account page; a team can be
too. Departments are used as a target for communications.
