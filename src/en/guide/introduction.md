# Overview

M'City IQ is the internal platform of M'City IQ Ltd. It brings together three
spaces, reached with a single account:

- **the public site** ([mcityiq.com](https://mcityiq.com)): M'City, its products
  and its zones, and a contact form;
- **the intranet**: your desk, communications, shared resources and a space for
  each product;
- **the M'City back-office**: the product and zone registry, accounts, site
  content, supervision and configuration, depending on your position.

The **products** M'City runs, such as Auticway, each have their own
back-office. M'City IQ never handles their operations: it opens them for you
through single sign-on (SSO) when you are granted access.

## Key notions

| Notion                | Definition                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Position              | Your job profile, one of seven. It decides which **functions** you see. An account has one active position.                  |
| Scope                 | The products, zones and offices assigned to your account. It decides which **data** you see in those functions.              |
| Team                  | A group of named accounts responsible for one or more products. An account can belong to several teams.                      |
| Zone                  | A specific urban or peri-urban area. Only a **qualified** zone can be attached to a product.                                 |
| Office                | A physical M'City location, tied to a zone.                                                                                  |
| Department            | A unit of the organization, used to target communications.                                                                   |
| Product back-office   | A product's own administration tool (e.g. the Auticway back-office).                                                         |
| SSO                   | Single sign-on: your M'City account opens the intranet, the M'City back-office and the product back-offices you are granted. |
| General administrator | The CEO, or a delegated account: invites accounts and manages the configuration.                                             |

## Languages

The interface is available in English and French. You choose your working
language in [My account](./account#language); it applies to the whole intranet
and to your notifications. Published content (communications, resources, site
pages) is entered in English and French; when the French is missing, the
English text is shown.
