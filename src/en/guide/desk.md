# The desk

The intranet is open to every active account. The left menu groups, under
**Intranet**, your desk, communications and resources; under
**Administration**, the functions of your position (absent if your position has
none). **View site** opens the public site.

Everything you see is filtered by your [scope](./introduction#key-notions).

## My desk

The intranet home page shows:

- **My scope**: your products, zones and offices ("All products and all zones"
  for management);
- **Latest communications** and **Recent resources**;
- **Product spaces**: a way into each product of your scope;
- alert banners depending on your position: new contact messages to handle,
  desynchronized products.

If no position is assigned to you yet, the desk says so: ask the general
administrator to complete your account.

## Communications

**Communications** lists the announcements that concern you: those addressed to
all of M'City, and those targeted at your position, your department, a product,
zone or office of your scope, or at you by name. Each communication shows its
author, date and target.

A communication is written in a single language, chosen by its author.

## Resources

**Resources** gathers shared documents, procedures, notes, contacts and links.
Filter by type; open a link or download a document.

- A **general** resource is visible to every account.
- A resource tied to a product is visible only if your scope covers that
  product; it also appears in the product's space.

## Product spaces

Each active product of your scope has its space. It shows:

- the zones where the product operates;
- the responsible teams and their members;
- the product's own communications and resources;
- **View the public page**: its landing page on the site;
- **Open the back-office**: its back-office, without entering your credentials
  again.

The **Open the back-office** button appears only if you are granted access,
account by account or because you belong to a team responsible for the
product. Otherwise the space says so; ask the general administrator. Having a
product in your scope is not enough to open its back-office.

## Opening a product back-office

1. Open the product's space, then **Open the back-office**.
2. The back-office signs you in with your M'City account (a "Sign in with
   M'City" button on its sign-in page, if you go there directly).
3. If you are not signed in to M'City yet, the M'City sign-in (and its two-factor
   authentication) comes first.

Signing out of M'City also closes the back-office sessions opened from that
session. What you can do in a back-office depends on your position: for
Auticway, see [Auticway back-office](./auticway).
